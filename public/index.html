Features section heading

Vinted Heist offers a few features that put our platform above our competitors.

Avg price calculation

Our dashboard allows you to calculate the average reselling price for an item on vinted. This allows you to pick an appropriate price to get notifications. Eg. cortiez hoodie at 30£ average price, you can get notis when it is listed under 10£ and buy instantly.

Notifications

It will send you notifications whenever a listing is detected under your price threshhold. These are sent out, under ~2 minutes after an item is listed. This should mean you are the first buyer to notice the listing, meaning you can get the best prices.

The platform uses servers worldwide to constantly get the newest listings for customers like you. As soon as somebody posts something, our scanners will be for sure detecting the listing asap.

Versatile Design.
